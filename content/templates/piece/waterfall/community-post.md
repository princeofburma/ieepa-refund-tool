# Community post  (the most underused surface — do this first)

YouTube distributes community posts on the homepage; they can be ~10% of channel
impressions. Treat the post as **a unit of content in itself**, not an ad.

**Rule:** never "here's my new video, go watch." That's the worst way to promote
anything — the viewer has already seen it, or feels spammed (principle 7).

## Format that works
Single image + hook text (simplest, performs well), or a short carousel of the
surplus value from `02-packaging.md`. Don't crowd the text; lead with a hook.
